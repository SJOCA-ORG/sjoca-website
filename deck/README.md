# deck/

The Parish Council proposal slides — Step 1 of [`../PROJECT-PLAN.md`](../PROJECT-PLAN.md).

Empty until we draft it.

Two things to keep in mind when working here:

**No technical content.** No tool names beyond ChurchTrac (which the council already
knows), no hosting mechanics, no jargon. The council is deciding *whether* and *what*.
*How* is ours, and comes later.

**The analysis lives in `../docs/survey-findings.md`, not in the slides.** Slides carry
the argument; the document carries the reasoning behind it. Keeping them separate means
the thinking survives past the meeting.

The deck outline — what each slide covers and what we're asking for — is in Step 1 of the
project plan.
