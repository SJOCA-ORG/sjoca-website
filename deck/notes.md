# Notes for the slides

**Started 20 October 2026.** Mitch dictates, I write down. Raw material for a
deck, not the deck itself — the deck carries the argument, this carries
everything we might want to argue from.

Two columns throughout: **what is done** and **what is still to do**. Keep them
honest. A list of only wins is not usable at a meeting where people have been
waiting a year for the schedule to be right.

See [`README.md`](README.md) for the rule about what may appear on a slide:
no tool names beyond ChurchTrac, no hosting mechanics, no jargon.

## Opening note — say this first

Mitch's words, for the beginning of the presentation (evening of 20 October 2026):

> This is not the final or approved version. This is only to show progress.
> Please do not worry about typos in titles, or names — part of what I will show
> you tonight is how easy it will be to fix these problems going forward.

Worth making this its own opening slide rather than a spoken aside. It sets the
frame for everything after it: every typo someone spots becomes a demonstration
instead of a defect.
